## Q1: What engineering branches are available?

### Engineering Branches Available

Our demo college offers the following six undergraduate engineering branches:

1. **Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)**
   - Focuses on programming, software development, algorithms, operating systems, and computer networks.
2. **Information Technology (IT)**
   - Covers web development, databases, cloud computing, cybersecurity, and information systems.
3. **Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)**
   - Includes digital electronics, communication systems, embedded systems, and microprocessors.
4. **Mechanical Engineering (ME)**
   - Covers machine design, manufacturing, thermodynamics, and robotics.
5. **Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI & DS)**
   - Focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and intelligent applications.
6. **Electrical Engineering (EE)**
   - Includes electrical machines, power systems, control systems, and electrical circuit design.

**Choosing a branch:** Consider your interests, preferred subjects, curriculum, and career goals before making a decision.

## Q2: Explain the admission process.

### Step-by-Step Admission Process

1. **Check eligibility:** Review the academic qualifications and entrance examination requirements for your selected course.
2. **Complete registration:** Fill out the application form with your personal and academic information.
3. **Prepare documents:** Collect mark sheets, identity proof, photographs, and other required certificates.
4. **Take the entrance examination:** Complete the applicable examination and obtain the required score or rank.
5. **Participate in counselling:** Follow the applicable merit-based selection and counselling process.
6. **Seat allotment:** Select or accept an available branch according to your merit, preferences, and eligibility.
7. **Document verification:** Submit the required documents for verification.
8. **Pay admission fees:** Complete the payment and retain the receipt.
9. **Confirm admission:** Follow the instructions for reporting, orientation, and commencement of classes.

**Tip:** Keep digital and physical copies of your application and payment receipts.

## Q3: Tell me about eligibility criteria.

### Eligibility Criteria for Engineering Admission

For this fictional demonstration, assume the following requirements:

1. **Educational qualification:** Passed Class 12 or an equivalent examination from a recognized board.
2. **Required subjects:** Physics and Mathematics, together with other subjects prescribed for the selected engineering course.
3. **Minimum marks:** 45% aggregate for general-category applicants and 40% for eligible reserved-category applicants.
4. **Entrance examination:** A valid score in the applicable engineering entrance examination may be required.
5. **Document verification:** Academic certificates and applicable eligibility certificates must be submitted.
6. **Seat availability:** Admission depends on merit, applicable rules, counselling, and available seats.

Actual eligibility rules can differ by institution, course, admission year, and regulations. The percentages above are fictional values for demo testing.

## Q5: What documents are required for admission?

### Admission Document Checklist

Prepare the following documents as applicable:

1. Class 10 mark sheet and passing certificate.
2. Class 12 mark sheet and passing certificate.
3. Entrance examination scorecard.
4. Entrance examination admit card, if required.
5. School or college leaving certificate.
6. Transfer certificate, if applicable.
7. Government-issued identity proof.
8. Recent passport-size photographs.
9. Domicile certificate, if required.
10. Caste certificate and caste validity certificate, where applicable.
11. Non-creamy layer certificate or other applicable eligibility certificates, if required.
12. Migration certificate, if applicable.
13. Seat allotment letter or admission confirmation document.

**Before submitting your documents:**

- Verify that your name and date of birth match across records.

## Q6: What scholarships can students apply for?

### Scholarships and Financial Assistance

Students may explore the following scholarship categories in this fictional demo:

1. **Merit-based scholarships** — For students with strong academic performance.
2. **Need-based financial assistance** — For students who demonstrate financial need.
3. **Reserved-category scholarships** — For eligible students under applicable schemes.
4. **Minority scholarships** — For students meeting requirements of relevant schemes.
5. **Government scholarships** — For eligible students under relevant central or state programs.
6. **Special achievement awards** — For recognized achievements in academics, sports, innovation, or other approved activities.

**Common application requirements**

- Academic mark sheets
- Identity proof
- Income certificate, if required
- Applicable category certificate
- Bank account details
- Admission proof and fee receipt

**Application process**

1. Identify the scholarship category.
2. Review the eligibility requirements.
3. Collect the required documents.
4. Submit the application before the applicable deadline.
5. Track the application and respond to verification requests.

Eligibility, scholarship amounts, and deadlines depend on the applicable scheme.
