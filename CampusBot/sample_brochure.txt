CAMPUS UNIVERSITY OFFICIAL ADMISSION BROCHURE & GUIDELINES (2026-2027)
========================================================================

Welcome to Campus University! This document contains the official admission criteria, deadlines, courses, tuition fees, scholarships, and guidelines.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. COURSES & DEGREES OFFERED
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Campus University offers high-quality undergraduate programs across multiple domains:

* Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science & Engineering
  - Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters)
  - Intake: 180 seats

* Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)
  - Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)
  - Intake: 120 seats

* Bachelor of Science (B.Sc) in Biotechnology
  - Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)
  - Intake: 60 seats

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ADMISSION ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Prospective students must meet the following criteria to qualify for admissions:

* For B.Tech (Computer Science & Engineering):
  - Candidates must have completed High School (12th Grade or equivalent) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) as core subjects.
  - A minimum score of 60% aggregate in PCM is required.
  - Candidates must qualify in the Campus Engineering Entrance Test (CEET).

* For BBA:
  - Candidates must have completed High School (12th Grade or equivalent) in any stream (Science, Commerce, or Arts).
  - A minimum score of 50% aggregate is required.

* For B.Sc (Biotechnology):
  - Candidates must have completed High School (12th Grade or equivalent) with Biology as a mandatory subject.
  - A minimum score of 55% aggregate in Biology is required.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FEE STRUCTURE (TUITION FEES)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The annual tuition fees for various programs are as follows:

* B.Tech Computer Science: $5,000 per year (payable in two equal installments of $2,500 at the start of each semester).
* BBA: $3,500 per year.
* B.Sc Biotechnology: $3,000 per year.

Note: Examination fees of $150 per semester are charged extra.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL AID
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Campus University is committed to supporting talented and deserving students through the following scholarship programs:

* merit-Based Scholarship (Academic Excellence):
  - 50% tuition fee waiver for students who scored above 95% aggregate in High School.
  - 25% tuition fee waiver for students who scored between 90% and 94.9% aggregate in High School.

* Financial-Need / Income-Based Scholarship:
  - 25% tuition fee waiver for students whose family annual income is below $3,000.
  - Requirements: A valid Income Certificate issued by the competent government authority must be submitted.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS FOR ADMISSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
At the time of admission confirmation, candidates must submit the following original documents:

1. High School Graduation Marksheet and Certificate (10th and 12th Grade).
2. Transfer Certificate (TC) or Leaving Certificate from the last attended school.
3. Government-issued Photo Identification (e.g., Passport, National ID card).
4. 4 recent Passport-size photographs.
5. Income Certificate (only applicable if applying for the Financial-Need Scholarship).
6. Entrance Exam Score Card (applicable only for B.Tech applicants).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. HOSTEL FACILITIES & HOSTEL FEES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
We offer safe and modern hostel facilities for boys and girls inside the campus:

* Single Occupancy Room (Air Conditioned): $1,800 per year (includes room rent and internet).
* Shared Occupancy Room (Double, Non-AC): $1,200 per year.
* Mess / Dining Charges: Included in the hostel fees, providing 3 nutritious meals daily.
* Security Deposit: A refundable deposit of $200 is charged at the time of allotment.
